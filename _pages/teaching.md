## Teaching Experience

### CMED7330: Introduction to AI in Healthcare
**Li Ka Shing Faculty of Medicine, The University of Hong Kong (HKU)**

**Role:** Lecturer  
**Terms:** Spring 2025, Spring 2026

This graduate-level course introduces artificial intelligence concepts and their applications in healthcare. As one of the lecturers, I am specifically responsible for the modules on **AI Ethics, Data Privacy, and Security**, guiding students through the critical ethical and regulatory landscapes of medical AI.

**My Teaching Focus:**
- **AI Ethics & Bias:** Addressing algorithmic fairness, bias mitigation in medical datasets, and ethical decision-making in AI-driven care.
- **Data Privacy & Security:** Exploring privacy-preserving techniques (such as Federated Learning), data governance, and compliance with healthcare regulations (e.g., PDPO, HIPAA).
- **Regulatory Frameworks:** Understanding the legal and ethical standards for deploying AI tools in clinical settings.

**Course Overview:**
The broader course covers machine learning fundamentals, medical image analysis, natural language processing for clinical text, and predictive analytics. My modules ensure that students not only understand the technical capabilities of AI but also the crucial responsibilities regarding patient data protection and ethical implementation.

**Teaching Responsibilities:**
- Delivered specialized lectures on ethical challenges and privacy solutions in healthcare AI.
- Designed case studies focusing on real-world ethical dilemmas and privacy breaches.
- Guided student projects to ensure adherence to ethical guidelines and privacy-preserving best practices.
